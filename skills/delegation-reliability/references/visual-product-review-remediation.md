# Visual product-review remediation

Use only for runnable UI/design pull requests that claim product or design readiness. Do not require visual evidence for non-visual controller or backend-only changes.

Exercise the primary journey, progress and interruption/resume states, result edges, trust/provenance claims, required responsive viewports, accessibility, feedback disclosure, and evidence freshness. Bind regenerated screenshots and browser evidence to the final exact SHA; screenshot counts, build output, and width checks are not product approval.

Freeze the reviewed parent identity and give one child every material finding. Require a pushed child PR against that parent. If no PR exists, classify the handoff undelivered, confirm the worker stopped, recover declared residue safely, or reconstruct from the immutable parent. Verify live base/head, ancestry, paths, and mergeability; then run fresh exact-SHA product review. After an approved child merges, refresh and re-review the changed aggregate parent—child approval never transfers.
