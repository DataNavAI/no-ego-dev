# No-marketing side-income opportunity filter

Use this reference when researching ethical side-income opportunities for an operator who will not personally market the product. This is an applicability branch of the core evidence workflow, not permission to skip demand, competitor, trust, or feasibility research.

## Decision rule

Do not recommend an idea until its likely discovery channel is named and supported by evidence. “Build a landing page” is not a distribution plan when the operator will not do outbound sales, social posting, paid ads, influencer outreach, or ongoing content.

## Channel-first opportunity classes

Prefer opportunities with a credible product-led discovery surface:

- Browser extensions with store-search intent.
- App or plugin marketplaces such as Chrome, VS Code, Obsidian, Shopify, WordPress, Slack, or Notion when relevant to the target user.
- SEO micro-tools where the tool immediately answers a recurring query.
- Paid exports or pro unlocks for utilities that can run client-side or with bring-your-own-key infrastructure.
- Established marketplaces only when a specific niche has visible demand and weak or stale listings.

Downgrade these unless independent evidence establishes another credible channel:

- Generic templates.
- Consulting lead-generation pages.
- Broad AI wrappers with no distribution wedge.
- Products that require manual service delivery to create revenue.
- Products that overlap the operator's active business or could commoditize it.

## Evidence before selection

For each candidate, gather:

- store or marketplace search results and relevant competitor density;
- recent reviews, complaints, and update recency;
- public community, repository, or marketplace signals for adjacent products;
- search/autocomplete language, with locale and date when used;
- a concrete explanation of how discovery occurs without operator-led promotion; and
- an explicit non-overlap assessment against the operator's active business.

Do not turn absence of marketing effort into an assumption of organic traffic. A marketplace or SEO surface is credible only when query intent, result type, competition, and the product's immediate value support discovery.

## Shortlist output

For every shortlisted opportunity include:

- `distribution channel`;
- `why users can discover it without operator-led marketing`;
- `channel evidence and competitor update/review signals`;
- `active-business conflict check`; and
- `validation test`, including a go/no-go threshold that can be observed without requiring the operator to start the forbidden marketing activities.
