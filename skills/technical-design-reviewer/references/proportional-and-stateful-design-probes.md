# Proportional and stateful design probes

## Proportional low-risk MVP

Treat user/revenue numbers only as configurable policy examples. Eligibility requires exact isolated non-user-data scope, owner authority, credible recovery, readable preflight, exact allowlist and protected denylist, stop-on-mismatch, no blind retry, and independent final readback. Identity, credentials, billing, regulated/user/shared data, publication/deployment, irreversible mutation, and weak recovery stay on the full-risk path.

Probe every bounded sibling/ancestor path and include safe positive controls. A detector that rejects all inputs is not safe.

## Archive and authority continuity

Bind candidate/base archives and hashes, exact artifact digest, all prior report digests, stable dispositions, remediation paths, and context digest. Enumerate all independently current/normative/blocking indexes, runbooks, handoffs, release, migration, and product contracts; align or explicitly historical-scope each one.

A handoff digest is authoritative only if the consumer receives explicit source bytes/object, applies declared canonical serialization and omission rules, recomputes, and compares it. Probe substitution, omitted input, and coordinated metadata replacement.

## Capacity and outage probes

Compute capacity from maximum legal encoded item sizes multiplied by required rows and rounded provider units. Include pagination, summary/readback, retries, maintenance, operator paths, monthly envelope, and reserve. Keep numerical thresholds configurable to the governing design.

For every outage-independence claim, trace nonce, intent, CAS, key/signature lookup, receipt, provider token, and readback. Any mandatory dependency on the failed subsystem disproves the claim.

## Versioned/stateful remediation

Trace current, old-valid, retired, unknown, and wrong key versions through live keys, verifiers, indexes, tombstones, key retention, and migration. Probe TTL-expired physical rows refreshed across a count/CAS boundary.

Persist desired intent revision, request identity/phase, attempt, confirmed/unknown state, and revision-bound completion. Test response loss, restart, stale completion, enable/disable reorder, revoke-before-delayed-enable, and bounded/manual confirmation. Never convert unconfirmed server state into confirmed absence.

## Durable evidence architecture

Prove each terminal enum has a legal authority and transaction trace. Segment aggregates across every identity-bearing generation. Keep scheduler eligibility distinct from actual trigger existence. External publication must converge after accepted/local-response-lost ambiguity through provider idempotency or explicit adjudication. Evidence must survive the originating worker and remain independently queryable.
