# Benchmark Notes

The benchmark product is a small intake-first landing page for an appointment-based local service. It is effective because it turns a messy first message into a structured service brief.

Useful patterns to preserve:

- A concise hero that names the target professional and the job-to-be-done.
- A short client intake form with only the fields needed for a first decision.
- A live generated summary that shows what the service provider can act on.
- Lightweight trust cues and next-step copy.
- Responsive, publishable styling that works as a public test page.

Do not copy:

- the benchmark's exact brand name,
- its exact copy,
- irrelevant service-specific fields,
- a full production backend unless the request explicitly needs one.
